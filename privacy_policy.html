Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

TheHouseWins Scanner

TheHouseWins Scanner is a private application used to analyse publicly available eBay UK listing information for research and resale decision-making.

Information We Access

When authorised by an eBay account holder, the application may access information made available through eBay APIs, including information necessary to operate the application and analyse eBay listings.

The application does not sell, rent, or trade eBay user information.

Use of Information

Information obtained through eBay APIs is used only to provide the functionality of TheHouseWins Scanner, including listing research, market analysis, valuation and related decision-support features.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

The application does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information from eBay users.

Third Parties

The application uses eBay APIs to obtain information required for its functionality. Use of eBay services is also subject to eBay's applicable terms and privacy notice.

Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application credentials and information accessed through the eBay APIs.

Contact

For privacy questions regarding TheHouseWins Scanner, contact the application owner through the contact details associated with the application.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the application or applicable requirements.

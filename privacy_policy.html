TheHouseWins Scanner Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

TheHouseWins Scanner is a private tool used to monitor and analyse eBay listings for research, valuation and resale decision-making.

Information We Access

When authorised by the user, TheHouseWins Scanner may access information provided through eBay's APIs. This may include publicly available listing information and account information required for the operation of the application.

How Information Is Used

Information accessed through eBay is used only to operate the application, analyse eBay listings, perform market research and provide information to the authorised user.

The application does not sell, rent or provide users' eBay information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

TheHouseWins Scanner only retains information where necessary for the operation of the application. Access credentials and OAuth tokens are treated as confidential and are not intentionally made publicly available.

Third-Party Services

The application uses eBay APIs to obtain eBay-related information. Use of eBay services is also subject to eBay's own terms and privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information used by the application. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

User Control

Users may revoke the application's access to their eBay account through their eBay account settings.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be made available on this webpage.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or TheHouseWins Scanner, please contact the application owner.
